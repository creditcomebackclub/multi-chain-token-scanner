# Scalp v2 rollout note — 2026-09-25

## Why the rule changed

The five most recent v1 chart alerts contained only two tokens, including four separate AKE alerts. Three later AKE entries were 5.6%–9.8% above the identified support zone with 11%–15% structural risk; each reached its stop before the old +50% target. The target and entry timing did not match a five-minute scalp.

## New live rule

- Refresh the eligible watchlist hourly while preserving an armed support setup.
- Require a green support reclaim with at least 1.5x the preceding 20-candle volume.
- Require EMA9 above EMA21, price above SMA50, and a rising EMA9.
- Reject entries more than 3% above support or with more than 8% structural risk.
- Expire an entry ten minutes after its signal.
- Sell-plan guidance: 50% at +5%, move the remainder's stop to entry, and take the remainder at +10%.
- Continue exact-pool DEX Screener validation and a GoPlus `PASS` before Telegram delivery.

The service sends alerts and plans only. It does not place orders or exits.

## Research evidence and its limit

The candidate rule produced ten signals across 14 currently discoverable pairs and roughly 41 hours of five-minute candles. At a +5% target, seven reached target before stop, two reached stop first, and one remained open. Applying the existing 24-hour token cooldown reduced the practical sample to about six alerts.

This was a small retrospective sample drawn from a current trending-pair universe. It has selection and survival bias and is suitable only as a candidate for forward measurement. It is not evidence of future profitability.

## Data-plan decision

Keep public discovery because it costs no paid credits. If the public candle route remains rate-limited, use CoinGecko Basic only for chart candles. Ten charts every five minutes require about 86,400 successful REST calls per 30 days, which fits the plan's stated 100,000 monthly credits with about 13,600 calls of headroom. Twenty charts at that interval would require about 172,800 calls and would not fit.

Bitquery Pro is not the first purchase for this chart strategy: the existing all-trade, five-chain stream can consume its 5 GB allowance quickly, while the weak v1 entries were a strategy problem rather than a missing firehose problem.

## Rollback

Disable chart messages immediately with `CHART_SETUPS_ENABLED=false`. The new rule name changes alert identities, so old v1 records remain available for comparison and are not overwritten.
